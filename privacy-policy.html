Privacy Policy

A safe space for couples

Effective date: May 20, 2025  ·  Last updated: May 20, 2025

01 Overview

SafeSpace is a private communication app designed exclusively for couples. It provides a calm, structured environment to share feelings and resolve moments of tension with care and intention.

Your privacy is the foundation of everything SafeSpace is built on. This policy explains clearly what we collect, why we collect it, and how it is protected.

By using SafeSpace, you agree to the terms described in this policy. If you have any questions, you can reach us at the contact details below.

02 Information We Collect

SafeSpace collects only the minimum information needed to operate the app:

  • Your first name — entered by you when you first set up the app. Used only to identify you within your private room.
  • Room code — a short code used to connect two partners in a private shared space.
  • Messages and mood selections — the content you send within your room, including mood indicators and written messages.
  • Push notification token — a device identifier used solely to deliver notifications to your partner when you send a message.
  • Timestamps — the date and time of messages and resolutions, used to manage time-lock features within the app.

We do not collect your email address, phone number, location, or any other personal identifier.

03 How We Use Your Information

All information collected is used exclusively to provide the SafeSpace experience:

  • To connect you and your partner in a private, two-person room
  • To display messages and moods within your shared inbox
  • To send push notifications to your partner when you share a moment
  • To manage the resolution flow and time-lock features
  • To maintain your session so you don't have to re-enter your details

Your data is never used for advertising, analytics profiling, or any commercial purpose.

04 Data Storage & Security

SafeSpace uses Supabase — a secure, hosted database platform — to store room and message data. Supabase applies industry-standard encryption and security practices.

Your session data (name, room code, push token) is stored locally on your device using Expo SecureStore, which uses your device's secure storage system.

Only you and your partner can access the messages in your room. No one else — including the developer — can read your private conversations.

Row Level Security (RLS) is enabled on all database tables, ensuring that each room's data is isolated and protected.

05 Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties for marketing or commercial purposes.

The only third-party services involved in operating SafeSpace are:

  • Supabase — database and real-time messaging infrastructure
  • Expo / Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) — push notification delivery

These services are used solely to power SafeSpace's core features and operate under their own privacy policies.

06 Data Retention

Your room and messages remain stored as long as your room exists in the app. Resolved moments are archived within your room and remain accessible to both partners.

If you would like your data deleted, please contact us at the email below and we will remove your room and all associated data promptly.

07 Children's Privacy

SafeSpace is designed for adults in romantic relationships. The app is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

08 Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Know what data is stored about you
  • Request deletion of your data at any time
  • Stop using the app and have your information removed

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at adelattef@gmail.com and we will respond within 7 days.
